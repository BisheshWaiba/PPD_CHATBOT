How it works

Three steps, one gentle conversation.

01

Talk it through

Sutkeri asks a few gentle questions, one at a time. Reply in your own words, or just type a number.

02

Take a short screening

Answer 10 short questions from the Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS), a widely used screening tool for postpartum depression. Choose English or Nepali, and get a score with plain-language guidance.

03

Share it with your doctor

Get a printable results page with your score, your answers and where to find help, ready for your next appointment.

Why Sutkeri

Always there. Always private.

24/7 AI support

Ask a question whenever it comes up, at 3 a.m. or mid-afternoon. The assistant is always on and never rushes you.

Self-care tips

Ask for simple, practical tips on rest, food, water and accepting help while you recover.

Private to you

Your screening answers are saved only in your own browser and cleared when you log out. Your password is never stored in readable form.

Your report

A clear summary you can hand to your doctor.

When you finish the questionnaire, Sutkeri turns your answers into a results page. Print it, or show it at your next appointment.

  • Your score out of 30
  • What the score means, in plain language
  • Your answers, question by question
  • Where to get help, including Nepal helplines
