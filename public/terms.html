2. What Sutkeri is, and what it is not

Sutkeri is a companion for the weeks and months after a birth. It offers a place to talk, general information, and a short questionnaire (the Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale) that can show how you have been feeling over the past week.

It is not medical care. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. The questionnaire is a screening: a score does not say that you do or do not have depression, and a score below the usual cut-off does not mean you are well. Nothing here replaces a doctor, midwife, nurse, counsellor or other health professional. Please speak to one about anything that worries you, and do not delay or ignore their advice because of something you read here.

The chat will show them, but it cannot make sure you see them.
